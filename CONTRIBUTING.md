# Contributing to OpenGA

Thanks for your interest — this is a public, open initiative, and
contributions are welcome.

## License & contributions

OpenGA is released under the Apache-2.0 License. Contributions are
made by individuals; you keep copyright to your work and license it
under the same terms by contributing. Feel free to note your
affiliation in commit history or papers — that describes you, not
the project.

## How to contribute

Fork, branch off `main`, keep one concern per PR, and open it against `main`.

## Questions

Open an issue.
