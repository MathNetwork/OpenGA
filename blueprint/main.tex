\noindent
This document is the mathematical blueprint for the Lean formalization of the
Hopf--Rinow theorem in \texttt{OpenGALib}. It transcribes the chapters of do
Carmo, \emph{Riemannian Geometry}, that lie on the dependency path to the theorem:
differentiable manifolds (Ch.~0), Riemannian metrics (Ch.~1), affine and
Riemannian connections (Ch.~2), geodesics and convex neighborhoods (Ch.~3), and
complete manifolds and the Hopf--Rinow / Hadamard theorems (Ch.~7). Nodes carry
\texttt{\textbackslash lean\{...\}} / \texttt{\textbackslash leanok} markers
recording their correspondence with the Lean library.
