title: Corollary 2.5

Let $\gamma:[0,a]\to M$ be a geodesic and put $p=\gamma(0)$. Then a Jacobi field
$J$ along $\gamma$ with $J(0)=0$ is given by

$$
J(t)=(d\exp_p)_{t\gamma'(0)}(tJ'(0)),\quad t\in[0,a].
$$
