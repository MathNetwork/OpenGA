Let $x\in {\mathcal M}$ with $\mathbf{t}(x)=t$ and with $R(x)=r^{-2}\ge r_{i+1}^{-2}$. Then there is $\kappa>0$ depending only
on $C$ such that ${\mathcal M}$ is $\kappa$-non-collapsed at $x$; that is to say,
if $R(x)=r^{-2}$ with $r\le r_{i+1}$, then $\mathit{Vol} B(x,t,r)\ge \kappa r^3$, or $x$ is contained in a component of $M_t$ with positive sectional curvature.
