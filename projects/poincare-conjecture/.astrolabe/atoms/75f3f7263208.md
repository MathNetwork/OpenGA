Fix $\kappa>0$ and $r_0>0$. We say that a Ricci flow with surgery
$({\mathcal M},G)$ is *$\kappa$-noncollapsed on scales $\le
r_0$* if the following holds for every point $x\in {\mathcal M}$ and
for every $r\le r_0$. Denote $\mathbf{t}(x)$ by $t$. If the parabolic
neighborhood $P(x,t,r,-r^2)$ exists in ${\mathcal M}$ and if
$|\mathit{Rm}_G|\le r^{-2}$ on $P(x,t,r,-r^2)$, then $\mathit{Vol} B(x,t,r)\ge \kappa r^3$.
