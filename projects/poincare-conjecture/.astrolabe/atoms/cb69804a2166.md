For any $0<\lambda<1$, the length of the ramp $\widetilde
\Gamma(c)^\lambda$ is at most $\lambda$ plus the length of
$\Gamma(c)$. The total curvature of $\widetilde\Gamma(c)^\lambda$ is
at most $n\pi$.
