The Hessian is a contravariant, symmetric two-tensor, i.e., for
 vector fields $X$ and $Y$ we have

$$
\mathit{Hess}(f)(X,Y)=\mathit{Hess}(f)(Y,X)
$$

and

$$
\mathit{Hess}(f)(\phi X,\psi Y)=\phi\psi \mathit{Hess}(f)(X,Y)
$$

for all smooth functions $\phi,\psi$. Other formulas for the Hessian
are

$$
\mathit{Hess}(f)(X,Y)=\langle \nabla_X(\nabla f),Y\rangle=\nabla_X(\nabla_Y(f))=\nabla^2f(X,Y).
$$

Also, in local coordinates we have

$$
\mathit{Hess}(f)_{ij}=\partial_i\partial_jf-(\partial_kf)\Gamma^k_{ij}.
$$
