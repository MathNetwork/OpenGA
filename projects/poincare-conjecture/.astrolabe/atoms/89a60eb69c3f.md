For each $r_{i+1}$ with $0<r_{i+1}\le r_i$, there is
$\delta=\delta(r_{i+1})>0$ (depending implicitly on $t_0$, $C$,
$\epsilon$, and $i$) such that if $\overline\delta(t)\le \delta$ for
all $t\in [T_{i-1},T_{i+1}]$ then the following holds. Let
$({\mathcal M},G)$ be a Ricci flow with surgery satisfying the
hypothesis of Proposition 16.1 with respect to the given
sequences and $r_{i+1}$, and let $x\in {\mathcal M}$ have $\mathbf{t}(x)=T$ with $T\in [T_i,T_{i+1})$. Suppose that for some $r\ge
r_{i+1}$ the parabolic neighborhood $P(x,r,T,-r^2)$ exists in
${\mathcal M}$ and $|\mathit{Rm}|\le r^{-2}$ on this neighborhood. Then
there is an open subset $U$ of $\mathbf{t}^{-1}[T_{i-1},T)$ contained
in the open subset of smooth manifold points of ${\mathcal M}$ with
the following properties:

- **(1)** For every $y$ in $U$ there is a minimizing ${\mathcal L}$-geodesic
connecting $x$ to $y$.
- **(2)** $U_t=U\cap \mathbf{t}^{-1}(t)$ is non-empty for every $t\in [T_{i-1},T)$
- **(3)** For each $t\in [T_{i-1},T)$ the restriction of ${\mathcal
L}$ to $U_t$ achieves its minimum and that minimum is at most
$3\sqrt{(T-t)}$.
- **(4)** The subset of $U$ consisting of all $y$ with the property that
${\mathcal L}(y)\le {\mathcal L}(y')$ for all $y'\in \mathbf{t}^{-1}(\mathbf{t}(y))$ has the property that its intersection with
$\mathbf{t}^{-1}(I)$ is compact for every compact interval $I\subset
[T_{i-1},T)$.
