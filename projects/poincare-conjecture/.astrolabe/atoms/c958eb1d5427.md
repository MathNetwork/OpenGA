For any ${\mathcal L}$-geodesic $\gamma$ parameterized by $[\tau_1,\bar\tau]$
we define

$$
K_{\tau_1}^{\bar\tau}(\gamma)=
\int_{\tau_1}^{\bar\tau}\tau^{3/2}H(X)d\tau.
$$

In the special case when $\tau_1=0$ we denote this integral by
$K^{\bar\tau}(\gamma)$.
