We say that a that a Ricci flow $(M,g(t))$ has *normalized
initial conditions*
if $0$ is the initial time for the flow and if the compact
Riemannian manifold $(M^n,g(0))$ satisfies:

1. $|\mathit{Rm}(x,0)|\le 1$ for all $x\in M$.
1. Let $\omega_n$ be the volume of the ball of radius $1$ in $n$ -dimensional Euclidean
space. Then $\mathit{Vol}(B(x,0,r))\ge (\omega_n/2)r^n$ for any $p\in
M$ and any $r\le 1$.

We also use the terminology $(M,g(0))$ *is normalized* to
indicate that it satisfies these two conditions.
