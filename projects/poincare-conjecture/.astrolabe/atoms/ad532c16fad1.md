For any $p\in N$ let $\lambda_1,\ldots,\lambda_{(n-1)(n-2)/2}$ be
the eigenvalues of $\mathit{Rm}_g(p)$. Then for any $s>0$ there are
$(n-1)$ zero eigenvalues of $\mathit{Rm}_{\tilde g}(p,s)$. The other
$(n-1)(n-2)/2$ eigenvalues of $\mathit{Rm}_{\tilde g}(p,s)$ are
$s^{-2}(\lambda_i-1)$.
