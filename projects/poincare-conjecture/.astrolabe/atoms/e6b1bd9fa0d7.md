**(Hamilton, cf.
[Hamilton3MPRC].)** Let $(M,g_0)$ be a compact
Riemannian manifold of dimension $n$.

1. There is a $T>0$ depending on $(M,g_0)$ and a Ricci flow
$(M,g(t)),\  0\le t<T$, with $g(0)=g_0$.
1. Suppose that we have Ricci flows with initial conditions $(M,g_0)$ at time
$0$ defined respectively on time intervals $I$ and $I'$. Then these
flows agree on $I\cap I'$.
