Let $0\le \tau_1<\tau_2$ be given and let $\gamma\colon
[\tau_1,\tau_2]\to {\mathcal M}$ be a continuous map. We say that
$\gamma$ is *parameterized by backward time* provided that $\gamma(\tau)\in M_{T-\tau}$ for all
$\tau\in [\tau_1,\tau_2]$
