For each $x\in U_n\subset M_n$ there is a flowline $\{x\}\times
(-R(x)^{-1},0]$ in ${\mathcal M}_n$. Furthermore, the scalar
curvature at any point of this flow line is less than or equal to
the scalar curvature at $x$.
