Fix $\epsilon>0$. Suppose that $(q,t)$ is a point in the standard
solution with $t\le R(q,t)^{-1}(1+\epsilon))$ and with $(q,0)\in
B(p_0,0,(\epsilon^{-1}/2)+A_0+5)$. Then $(q,t)$ is contained in an
$(C'(\epsilon),\epsilon)$-cap.
