Suppose that $0<\rho<r_0$ and that $\Omega^0$ is a component of
$\Omega$ which contains no point of $\Omega_\rho$. Then one of the
following holds:

- **(1)** $\Omega^0$ is a strong double $2\epsilon$-horn and is diffeomorphic to $S^2\times \Ar$.
- **(2)** $\Omega^0$ is a $2C$-capped $2\epsilon$-horn and is diffeomorphic to $\Ar^3$
or to a punctured $\Ar P^3$.
- **(3)** $\Omega^0$ is a compact component and is the union of the cores of
two $(2C,2\epsilon)$-caps and a strong $2\epsilon$-tube. It is
diffeomorphic to $S^3$, $\Ar P^3$ or $\Ar P^3\#\Ar P^3$.
- **(4)** $\Omega^0$ is a compact $2\epsilon$-round component and is diffeomorphic to a compact
manifold of constant positive curvature.
- **(5)** $\Omega^0$ is a compact component that fibers over $S^1$
with fibers $S^2$.
- **(6)** $\Omega^0$ is a compact
$2C$-component and is diffeomorphic to $S^3$ or to $\Ar P^3$.

See Fig.11.3.
