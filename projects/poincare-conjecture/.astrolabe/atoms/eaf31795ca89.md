We have

$$
w_{a,t'}(t'')=\mathit{exp}(-A(t''))\left(a-2\pi\int_{t'}^{t''}\mathit{exp}(A(t))dt\right).
$$

 If $a'>a$, then for $t_0\le t'<t''\le t_1$,
we have

$$
w_{a',t'}(t'')=w_{a,t'}(t'')+(a'-a)\mathit{exp}(-A(t'')).
$$
