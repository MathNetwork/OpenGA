Suppose that $(M,g(t)),\ a<t<b$ is a Ricci flow of non-negative Ricci curvature
with $M$ a connected manifold.
Then for any points $x,y\in M$ the function $d_{g(t)}(x,y)$ is a non-increasing function of $t$.
