We define
 the $\mathcal{L}$-*reduced length* (from $x$)

$$
l_x\colon {\mathcal U}_x\to \Ar
$$

by setting

$$
l_x(q)= \frac{L_x(q)}{2\sqrt{\tau}},
$$

 where $\tau=T-\mathbf{t}(q)$.
We denote by $l_x^\tau$ the restriction of $l_x$ to the slice ${\mathcal
U}_x(\tau)$.
