Let $\phi\colon B_{(q,t)}'\to \Ar$ be a non-negative, smooth
function with compact support. Then

$$
\int_{B_{(q,t)}'}\beta_{(q,t)}\triangle \phi d\mathit{vol}(g(t))\le \int_{(B_{(q,t)}')^*}\phi
\triangle \beta_{(q,t)} d\mathit{vol}(g(t)).
$$
