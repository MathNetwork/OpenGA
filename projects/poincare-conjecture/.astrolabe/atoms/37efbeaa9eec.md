Let $({\mathcal M},G)$ be a Ricci flow with surgery and for each $t$ set
$s(t)=s(M_t)$. If $t'<t$ then $s(t')\ge s(t)$. If we do surgery at time $t$
along at least one homotopically essential $2$-sphere, then $s(t)<s(t')$ for
any $t'<t$.
