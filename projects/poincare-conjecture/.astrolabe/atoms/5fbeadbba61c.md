For a standard solution $(\mathbb{R}^n,g(t)),\ 0\le t<T$, and for
any $t_0\in [0,T)$ there is a rotationally symmetric solution
$\psi_t(\mathbf{x)} = x e^{\tilde{\rho}(\mathbf{x},t)}$ to the harmonic
map flow

$$
\frac{\partial \psi_t}{\partial t}=\Delta_{g(t),g(t_0)}\psi(t)
\qquad \psi(t_0)(\mathbf{x})=\mathbf{x},
$$

and $ |\nabla^i \tilde{\rho}|(\mathbf{x},t)\leq \frac{C_{14}}{(1+|\mathbf{x}|)^2}$ for $0 \leq i \leq 3$ defined on some non-degenerate
interval $[t_0,T']$.
