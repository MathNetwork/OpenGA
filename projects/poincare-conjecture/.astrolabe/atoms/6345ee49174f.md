Consider the product $S^{2}\times \mathbb{R} $, with the metric $
g(t) =(1-t)g_{0}+ds^{2}.$ This is a $\kappa$-solution for any
$\kappa$ at most the volume of a ball of radius one in the product
of the unit $2$-sphere with $\Ar$.
