The curvature tensor $R_{ijkl}$, the Ricci curvature $\mathit{Ric}_{ij}$, the scalar
curvature $R$, and the volume form $d\mathit{vol}(x,t)$ satisfy the following evolution
equations under Ricci flow:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{\partial R_{ijkl}}{\partial t} &= & \triangle R_{ijkl} +2(B_{ijkl} -
B_{ijlk}-B_{iljk} + B_{ikjl})\nonumber \\&  &  - g^{pq}(R_{pjkl}\mathit{Ric}_{qi} +
R_{ipkl}\mathit{Ric}_{qj} + R_{ijpl}\mathit{Ric}_{qk} +
R_{ijkp}\mathit{Ric}_{ql})  \\
 \frac{\partial}{\partial t}\mathit{Ric}_{jk} & = & \triangle
\mathit{Ric}_{jk}+2g^{pq}g^{rs}R_{pjkr}\mathit{Ric}_{qs}-2g^{pq}\mathit{Ric}_{jp}\mathit{Ric}_{qk} \\
\frac{\partial}{\partial t}R & = & \Delta R+2|\mathit{Ric}|^2 \\
 \frac{\partial}{\partial t}d\mathit{vol}(x,t) & = &
-R(x,t)d\mathit{vol}(x,t).
\end{aligned}
$$
