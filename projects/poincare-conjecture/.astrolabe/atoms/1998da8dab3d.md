Let $(M,g)$ be a complete, connected, non-compact Riemannian manifold of
non-negative curvature. Fix a point $p\in M$. We define the *asymptotic
scalar curvature*

$$
{\mathcal R}(M,g)=\mathit{limsup}_{x\rightarrow\infty}R(x)d^2(x,p).
$$

 Clearly, this limit is
independent of $p$.
