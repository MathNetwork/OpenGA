Let $(M,g)$ be an $n$-dimensional complete Riemannian manifold and
fix $\kappa>0$. We say that $(M,g)$ is *$\kappa$-non-collapsed*
if for every $p\in M$ and any $r>0$, if $|\mathit{Rm}_g|\le r^{-2}$ on
$B(p,r)$ then $\mathit{Vol} B(p,r)\ge \kappa r^n$.
