Let $(M,g(t)),\ t_0\le t\le t_1$, be a Ricci flow on a compact
manifold. Suppose that $|\mathit{Ric}_{g(t)}|\le D$ for all $t\in
[t_0,t_1]$ Let $f\colon S^2\to (M,g(t_0))$ be a $C^1$-map. Then

$$
\mathit{Area}_{g(t_1)}f(S^2)\le \mathit{Area}_{g(t_0)}f(S^2)e^{4D(t_1-t_0)}.
$$
