Provided that $\delta>0$ is sufficiently small,
the angle (measured in $h(t)$) between $Y$ and $Z=(1,f_z)$ is
greater than $\pi/4$. Also,

- **(1)**

$$
k\le |Y|<\sqrt{2}k.
$$

- **(2)**

$$
\left|\langle \nabla_ZZ,Z\rangle\right|<(k+ 2\delta) |Z|^3.
$$

- **(3)**

$$
\left|\nabla_ZZ\right|<2(|Y|+\delta).
$$

- **(4)**

$$
|\langle Y,Z\rangle |\le |Y||f_z|(1+3\delta).
$$
