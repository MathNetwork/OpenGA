If $\gamma$ is a minimizing geodesic ray from $x$ to the end
${\mathcal E}$, then for any $y\in \gamma\setminus\{x\}$ the sub-ray
of $\gamma$ from $y$ to the end, is the unique shortest geodesic
from $y$ to the end.
