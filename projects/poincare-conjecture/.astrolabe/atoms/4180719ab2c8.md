For any $x$ in ${\mathcal M}$ with $\mathbf{t}(x)=T-\tau_1$ and any $Z\in T_xM_{t-\tau_1}$, there is $\delta>0$
such that for any $\tau$ with $\tau_1<\tau<\tau_1+\delta$ the map
${\mathcal L}\mathit{exp}_x^{\tau}$ is a local diffeomorphism from a
neighborhood of $Z$ in $T_xM_{T-\tau_1}$ to $M_{T-\tau}$.
