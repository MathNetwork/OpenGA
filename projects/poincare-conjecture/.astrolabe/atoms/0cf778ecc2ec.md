The *Laplacian operator* acting on scalar
functions on $M$ is given in local coordinates by

$$
\triangle  = \frac{1}{\sqrt{\mathit{det}(g)}}\partial_i
\left(g^{ij}\sqrt{\mathit{det}(g)}\partial_j\right).
$$
