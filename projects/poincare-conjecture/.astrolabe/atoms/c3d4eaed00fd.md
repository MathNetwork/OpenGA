Let $A\subset {\mathcal U}_x(\tau)$ be a measurable subset. Define
$\widetilde A\subset \widetilde{\mathcal U}_x(\tau)$ to be the
pre-image under ${\mathcal L}\mathit{exp}_x^{\tau}$ of $A$. Then

$$
\widetilde V_x(A)=\int_{\widetilde A}\tau^{-\frac{n}{2}}\mathit{exp}(-\tilde l(Z, \tau)){\mathcal J}(Z,\tau)dZ,
$$

 where $dZ$ is the usual
Euclidean volume element and ${\mathcal J}(Z,\tau)$ is the Jacobian determinant
of ${\mathcal L}\mathit{exp}_x^{\tau}$ at $Z\in T_xM_T$.
