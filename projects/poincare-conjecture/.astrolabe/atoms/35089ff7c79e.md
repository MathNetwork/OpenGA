Let $M$ be an $n$-dimensional manifold with $n$ being either $2$ or $3$.
If $(M,g)$ is Einstein with Einstein constant $\lambda$, one can
easily show that $M$ has constant sectional curvature
$\frac{\lambda}{n-1}$, so that in fact $M$ is a space-form.
