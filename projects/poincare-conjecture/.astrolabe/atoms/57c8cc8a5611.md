For each $A<\infty$ and for each integer $\ell\ge 0$, there is a
constant $C_2$ such that for all $n$ sufficiently large we have

$$
|\nabla^\ell{\mathit{Rm}}_{Q_nG_n}(x)|\le C_2
$$

 for all $x\in
B_{Q_nG_n}(x_n,0,A) $.
