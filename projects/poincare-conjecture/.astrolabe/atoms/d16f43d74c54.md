For any $q'\in M$ and any $0<\tau_0\le\tau'$ we have

$$
l_{x_k}(q',\tau') \ge -\frac{n}{2(\tau')^2}-\frac{\tau'}{2}-1+c_1\frac{d_{g^2_k(-\tau_0)}(q_k,q')}{\tau'},
$$

where $c_1$ is the constant from Lemma 9.25.
