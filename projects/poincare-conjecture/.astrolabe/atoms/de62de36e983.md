Using the metric, one can replace the Riemann curvature tensor
${\mathcal R}$ by a symmetric bilinear form $\mathit{Rm}$ on
$\wedge^2TM$. In local coordinates let
$\varphi=\varphi^{ij}\partial_i\wedge\partial _j$ and
$\psi=\psi^{kl}\partial _k\wedge\partial_l$ be local sections of
$\wedge^2TM$. The formula for $\mathit{Rm}$ is

$$
\mathit{Rm}(\varphi, \psi)=R_{ijkl}\varphi^{ij}\psi^{kl}.
$$

We call $\mathit{Rm}$ the *curvature
operator*. We say $(M,g)$ has
*positive curvature operator* if $\mathit{Rm}(\varphi,\varphi)>0$ for any nonzero 2-form $\varphi =
\varphi^{ij}\partial_{i}\wedge
\partial_{j}$ and has *nonnegative curvature operator* if
$\mathit{Rm}(\varphi,\varphi)\geq0$ for any $\varphi\in \wedge^2TM$.
