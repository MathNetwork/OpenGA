If $(M,g(t))$ is flat Euclidean $n$-space (independent of $t$), then
for any $x\in \Ar^n\times (-\infty,\infty)$ we have

$$
\widetilde V_x(M,\tau)=(4\pi)^{n/2}
$$

 for all $\tau>0$.
