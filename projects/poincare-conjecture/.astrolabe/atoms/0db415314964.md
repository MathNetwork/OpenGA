Let $(M,g(t)),\ 0\le t\le T$, be a Ricci flow with $M$ a compact,
connected $3$-manifold. Suppose that $\mathit{Rm}(x,0)\ge 0$ for all
$x\in M$. Then $\mathit{Rm}(x,t)\ge 0$ for all $x\in M$ and all $t\in
[0,T]$.
