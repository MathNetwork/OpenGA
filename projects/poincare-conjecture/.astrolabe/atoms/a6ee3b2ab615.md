There is a constant $c_1>0$ depending only on the dimension $n$ such
that for any $p,q\in M_k$ we have

$$
l_{x_k}(p,\tau)\ge -l_{x_k}(q,\tau)-1+c_1\frac{d^2_{g(-\tau)}(p,q)}{\tau}.
$$
