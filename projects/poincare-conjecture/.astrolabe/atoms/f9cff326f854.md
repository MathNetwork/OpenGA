Let $(M,g(t)),\ 0\le t<T<\infty$, be a Ricci flow with $M$ a compact manifold.
Then either the flow extends to an interval $[0,T')$ for some $T'>T$ or $|\mathit{Rm}|$ is unbounded on $M\times[0,T)$.
