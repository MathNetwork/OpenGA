The quotient $S^{2}\times \mathbb{R}/\langle\iota\rangle$, where the
involution $\iota$ is the product of the antipodal map on $S^2$ with
$s\mapsto -s$ on the $\Ar$ factor, is an orientable
$\kappa$-solution for some $\kappa>0$.
