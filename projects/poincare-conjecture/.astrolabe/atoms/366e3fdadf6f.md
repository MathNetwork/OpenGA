For any $t\in (-\infty,0)$ we have

$$
\mathit{lim}_{k\rightarrow\infty}|\nabla^{h_k} l_k|_{h_k}^2d\mathit{vol}(h_k)
=|\nabla l_\infty|_{g_\infty}^2 d\mathit{vol}(g_\infty)
$$

 in the sense
of distributions on $M_\infty\times \{t\}$.
