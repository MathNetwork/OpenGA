We define a function $\mathit{sn}_k$ as follows:

$$
\mathit{sn}_k(r)=\begin{cases}
r \ \  & \mathit{if\ \}k=0 \\
\frac{1}{\sqrt{k}}\mathit{sinh}(\sqrt{k}r) \ \ & \mathit{if \ \} k>0.
\end{cases}
$$
