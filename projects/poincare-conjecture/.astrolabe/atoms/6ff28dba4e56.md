For any $\epsilon>0$ there is $C'(\epsilon)<\infty$ such that for
any point $x$ in the standard solution one of the following holds
(see Fig. 12.2).

- **(1)** $(x,t)$ is contained in the core of a $(C'(\epsilon),\epsilon)$-cap.
- **(2)** $(x,t)$ is the center of an evolving $\epsilon$-neck $N$ whose initial time-slice is
$t=0$ and whose initial time-slice is disjoint from the surgery cap.
- **(3)** $(x,t)$ is the center of an evolving $\epsilon$-neck defined for rescaled time
$1+\epsilon$.
