Let $\gamma\colon [0,\tau_2]\to {\mathcal M}$ be an ${\mathcal L}$-geodesic.
Then $\mathit{lim}_{\tau\rightarrow 0}\sqrt{\tau}X_\gamma(\tau)$ exists. The
${\mathcal L}$-geodesic $\gamma$ is completely determined by this limit (and by
$\tau_2$).
