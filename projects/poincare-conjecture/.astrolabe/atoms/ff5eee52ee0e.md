For any $\tau>0$ and any $q$ we have

$$
|\nabla l_\infty(q,\tau)|\le
\frac{3}{\tau}d_{g_\infty}(-\tau)(q_\infty,q)+\sqrt{\frac{3}{\tau}}C_1(\tau),
$$

where $C_1(\tau)$ is the continuous function from
Corollary 9.16.
