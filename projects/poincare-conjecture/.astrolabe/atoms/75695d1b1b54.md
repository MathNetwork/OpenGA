The
Euler-Lagrange equation for critical paths for the $\mathcal{L}$-length is

$$
\nabla_{X}X - \frac{1}{2}\nabla R
+ \frac{1}{2\tau}X + 2\mathit{Ric}(X,\cdot)^* = 0.
$$
