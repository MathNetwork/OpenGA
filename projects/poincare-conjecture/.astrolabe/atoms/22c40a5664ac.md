*Surgery parameter sequences* are sequences

- **(i)** $\mathbf{r}=r_0\ge r_1\ge r_2\ge \cdots >0$, with $r_0=\epsilon$,
- **(ii)** $\mathbf{K}=\kappa_0\ge \kappa_1\ge \kappa_2\ge \cdots >0$
with $\kappa_0$ as in Claim 15.1, and
- **(iii)** $
\Delta=\delta_0\ge \delta_1\ge \delta_2\ge \cdots
>0$ with $\delta_0=\mathit{min}(\beta\epsilon/3,\delta_0',K^{-1},D^{-1})$
 where $\delta_0'$ is the constant
from Theorem 13.2 and $\beta<1/2$ is the constant from
Proposition 15.2, $\epsilon$ is the constant that we have
already fixed, and $K$ and $D$ are the constants from
Lemma 12.3.

We shall also refer to partial sequences defined for indices $0,\ldots, i$ for some $i>0$
as surgery parameter sequences if they are positive, non-increasing and if their initial
terms satisfy the conditions given above.
