The *Riemann curvature tensor* of $M$ is the $(1,3)$-tensor on $M$

$$
{\mathcal R}(X,Y)Z = \nabla^{2}_{X,Y}Z
- \nabla^{2}_{Y,X}Z = \nabla_{X}\nabla_{Y}Z-\nabla_{Y}\nabla_{X}Z
-\nabla_{[X,Y]}Z,
$$

 where
$\nabla^2_{X,Y}Z=\nabla_{X}\nabla_{Y}Z-\nabla_{\nabla_XY}Z$.
