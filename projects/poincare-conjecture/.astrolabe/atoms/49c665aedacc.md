Suppose that $\beta\colon [-a,a]\to \Ar$ is a continuous function
and that at each $s\in (-a,a)$ there is an upper barrier $\hat b_s$
for $\beta$ at $s$ with $\hat b_s''\le -3/2$. Then

$$
\frac{\beta(a)+\beta(-a)}{2}\le \beta(0)-\frac{3}{4}a^2.
$$
