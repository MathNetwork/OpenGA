For each $r<\infty$ there is a constant $C(r)<\infty$, such that the
following holds. Let $(M,g(t),(p,0))$ be a based $3$-dimensional
$\kappa$-solution satisfying $R(p,0)=1$. Then $R(q,0)\le C(r)$ for
all $q\in B(p,0,r)$.
