An *$n$-dimensional generalized Ricci flow* consists of a space-time ${\mathcal M}$ that is
$(n+1)$-dimensional and a horizontal metric $G$ satisfying the
generalized Ricci flow equation:

$$
{\mathcal L}_\chi(G)=-2\mathit{Ric}(G).
$$
