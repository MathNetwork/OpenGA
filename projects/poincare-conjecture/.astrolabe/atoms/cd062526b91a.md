Assume that $M$ is connected.
Given $p_1,p_2\in M$ and $0\le\tau_1<\tau_2\le T$, there is
 a minimizing ${\mathcal L}$-geodesic:
$\gamma\colon[\tau_{1},\tau_{2}]\to M\times [0,T]$ connecting
$(p_1,\tau_1)$ to $(p_2,\tau_2)$.
