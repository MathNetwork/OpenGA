Fix $\kappa>0$. Let $(M,g(t)),\ a<t\le b$, be a Ricci flow of
complete $n$-manifolds. Fix $r_0>0$. We say that $(M,g(t))$ is *$\kappa$-non-collapsed on scales at
most $r_0$* if the following holds for any $(p,t)\in M\times (a,b]$
and any $0<r\le r_0$ with the property that $a\le t-r^2$. Whenever
$|\mathit{Rm}(q,t')|\le r^{-2}$ for all $q\in B(p,t,r)$ and all $t'\in
(t-r^2,t]$, then $\mathit{Vol} B(p,t,r)\ge \kappa r^n$. We say that
$(M,g(t))$ is *$\kappa$-non-collapsed*, or equivalently *$\kappa$-non-collapsed on all scales* if it is
$\kappa$-non-collapsed on scales at most $r_0$ for every
$r_0<\infty$.
