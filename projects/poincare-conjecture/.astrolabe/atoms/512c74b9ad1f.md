The angle $\theta_B$ between $D'_x$ and $D'_y$ at the vertex $v$
satisfies

$$
\theta_B\le t_\xi+a(B)
$$

 and for any measurable subset
$B'\subset B$ we have

$$
\theta_B-t_\xi-a(B)\le \int_{B'}Kda<a(B).
$$
