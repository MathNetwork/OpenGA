Let $Q$ be an upper bound on $R(x,0)$ for all $x\in
M_\infty$. Then for any points $x,y\in M_\infty$ and any $t\in
(-T,0]$ we have

$$
d_{t}(x,y)\le d_0(x,y)+16\sqrt{\frac{Q}{3}}T.
$$
