Let $h_0$ be the round metric on $S^2$ with constant Gausssian
curvature $1/2$. Set $h(t)=(1-t)h_0$. Then the flow

$$
(S^2,h(t)),\
-\infty<t<1,
$$

 is a Ricci flow. We also have the product of this flow
with the trivial flow on the line: $(S^2\times \Ar,h(t)\times
ds^2),\ -\infty<t<1$. This is called the *standard shrinking
round cylinder*.
