Let $\gamma$ be a geodesic beginning at $p\in M$. For any $t>0$ we
say that $q=\gamma(t)$ is a *conjugate point along
$\gamma$* if there is a non-zero Jacobi field
along $\gamma$ vanishing at $\gamma(t)$.
