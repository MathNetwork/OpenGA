Let $M$ be a smooth manifold with a smooth vector field $\chi$ and a
smooth function $\mathbf{t}\colon M\to [a,b]$ with $\chi(\mathbf{t})=1$.
Suppose also that $F\colon M\to \Ar$ is a smooth function with the
properties:

1. for each $t_0\in [a,b]$ the restriction of $F$ to the level set $\mathbf{t}^{-1}(t_0)$ achieves its maximum, and
1. the subset ${\mathcal Z}$ of $M$
consisting of all $x$ for which $F(x)\ge F(y)$ for all $y\in \mathbf{t}^{-1}(\mathbf{t}(x))$ is a compact set.

 Suppose also that at each $x\in {\mathcal Z}$ we have $\chi(F(x))\le \psi(\mathbf{t}(x),F(x))$. Set $F_\mathit{max}(t)=\mathit{max}_{x\in \mathbf{t}^{-1}(t)}F(x)$. Then $F_\mathit{max}(t)$ is a continuous function and

$$
\frac{dF_\mathit{max}}{dt}(t)\le \psi(t,F_\mathit{max}(t))
$$

in the sense of forward difference quotients.
 Suppose that $G(t)$ satisfies the
differential equation

$$
G'(t)=\psi(t,G(t))
$$

 and has initial condition $F_\mathit{max}(a)\le G(a)$. Then for all $t\in [a,b]$ we have

$$
F_\mathit{max}(t)\le G(t).
$$
