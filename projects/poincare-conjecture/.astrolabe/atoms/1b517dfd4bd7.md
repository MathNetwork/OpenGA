Let $N$ be a Riemannian manifold of dimension $n-1$. Fix $(x,s)\in
c(N)=N\times (0,\infty)$. With respect to the coordinates
$(x^0,\ldots,x^n)$ the curvature operator ${\mathit{Rm}}_{\tilde
g}(p,s)$ of the cone decomposes as

$$
\begin{pmatrix} 0 & 0 \\ s^2(\mathit{Rm}_g(p)-\wedge^2g(p)) & 0
\end{pmatrix},
$$

where $\wedge^2g(p)$ is the symmetric form on $\wedge^2T_pN$ induced
by $g$.
