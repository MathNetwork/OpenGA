Given $\kappa>0$, there is $C<\infty$ such that for any
$3$-dimensional $\kappa$-solution $(M,g(t)),\ -\infty<t\le 0$, we
have

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathit{sup}_{(x,t)}\frac{|\nabla R(x,t)|}{R(x,t)^{3/2}}< C\\
 \mathit{sup}_{(x,t)}\frac{\left|\frac{d}{dt}R(x,t)\right|}{R(x,t)^2}<C.
\end{aligned}
$$
