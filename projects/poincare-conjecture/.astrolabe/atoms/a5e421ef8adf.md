For any tensor $\phi$ we have

$$
\nabla(\nabla_r\phi)=\nabla_r(\nabla\phi)+dx^k\otimes
\mathit{Rm}(\partial_k,\partial_r)(\phi)-\nabla_r(dx^l)\otimes
\nabla_l(\phi).
$$
