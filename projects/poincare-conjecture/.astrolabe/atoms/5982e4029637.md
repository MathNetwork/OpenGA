**(Uniformization Theorem)** If
$(M^n,g)$ is a complete, simply-connected Riemannian manifold of
constant sectional curvature $\lambda$, then:

1. If $\lambda=0$, then $M^n$ is isometric to Euclidean $n$-space.
1. If $\lambda>0$ there is a diffeomorphism $\phi \colon M \rightarrow S^{n}$
 such that g = $\lambda^{-1}\phi^{*}(g_\mathit{st})$ where $g_{st}$ is the usual metric on the unit sphere in $\Ar^{n+1}$.
1. If $\lambda<0$ there is a diffeomorphism $\phi \colon M \rightarrow {\mathbb H}^{n}$
 such that g = $\abs{\lambda}^{-1}\phi^{*}(g_\mathit{st})$ where $g_{st}$ is the Poincaré metric of constant curvature
$-1$ on ${\mathbb H}^n$.
