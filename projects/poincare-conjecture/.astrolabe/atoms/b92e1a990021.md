Let $c>0$ be the constant from Proposition 12.31. Fix
$0<\overline\delta_0<1/4$ such that if $g$ is within
$\overline\delta_0$ of $g_0$ in the $C^{[1/\overline
\delta]}$-topology then $|R_{g'}(x)-R_{g_0}(x)|<c/2$ and $|\mathit{Ric}_{g'}-\mathit{Ric}_{g_0}|<1/4$.
