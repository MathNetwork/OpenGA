Suppose that $(M,g)$ and $f\colon M\to \Ar$ satisfies the hypotheses of
Theorem 9.42 and that $M$ is compact and of positive curvature. Then
the Ricci flow $(M,G(t))$ with $G(-1)=g$ given in Equation (9.19) is a
shrinking family of compact round manifolds.
