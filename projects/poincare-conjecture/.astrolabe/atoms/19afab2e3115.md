There is an open neighborhood $U_p\subset T_pM$ of $0$ consisting
of all $v\in T_pM$ for which: (i) $\gamma_v$ is the unique minimal
geodesic from $p$ to $\gamma_v(1)$, and (ii) $ \mathit{exp}_p$ is a
local diffeomorphism at $v$. We set ${\mathcal C}_p\subset M$ equal
to $M\setminus \mathit{exp}_p(U_p)$. Then ${\mathcal C}_p$ is called
the *cut locus from $p$*. It is a closed subset
of measure $0$.
