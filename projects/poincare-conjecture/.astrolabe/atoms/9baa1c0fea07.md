A $\delta$-net in $(X,x)$ is a subset $L$ of $X$ containing $x$
whose $\delta$-neighborhood covers $X$ and for which there is some
$\delta'>0$ with $d(\ell_1,\ell_2)\ge \delta'$ for all $\ell_1\not=
\ell_2$ in $L$.
