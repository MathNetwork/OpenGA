For any $p\in M$, we can define the *exponential
map* at $p$, $\mathit{exp}_{p}$. It is
defined on an open neighborhood $O_{p}$ of the origin in $T_{p}M$
and is defined by $\mathit{exp}_{p}(v)=\gamma_v(1)$, the endpoint of
the unique geodesic $\gamma_v\colon [0,1]\rightarrow M$ starting
from $p$ with initial velocity vector $v$. We always take
$O_{p}\subset T_{p}M$ to be the maximal domain on which $\mathit{exp}_p$ is defined, so that $O_{p}$ is a star-shaped open
neighborhood of $0\in T_pM$. By the Hopf-Rinow Theorem, if $M$ is
complete, then the exponential map is define on all of $T_pM$.
