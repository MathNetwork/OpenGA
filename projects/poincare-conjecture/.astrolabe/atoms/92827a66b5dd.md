**(Pinching toward positive curvature)**
Let $(M,g(t)),\ 0\le t<T$, be a Ricci flow with $M$ a compact
$3$-manifold. Assume at for every $x\in M$, the eigenvalues,
$\lambda(x,0)\geq\mu(x,0)\geq\nu(x,0)$, of $\mathit{Rm}(x,t)$ are all
at least $-1$. Set $X(x,t)=\mathit{max}(-\nu(x,t),0)$. Then we have:

1. $R(x,t)\ge \frac{-6}{4t+1}$, and
1. for all $(x,t)$ for which $0<X(x,t)$

$$
R(x,t)\geq 2X(x,t)\left(\mathit{log}X(x,t)+\mathit{log}(1+t)-3\right).
$$
