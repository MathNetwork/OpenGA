Let $({\mathcal M},G)$ be a generalized Ricci flow whose domain of definition
is contained in $[0,\infty)$. Then we say that $({\mathcal M},G)$ has *curvature pinched toward positive* if
for every $x\in {\mathcal M}$ the following two conditions hold:

1.

$$
R(x)\ge \frac{-6}{4\mathbf{t}(x)+1}
$$

1.

$$
R(x)\geq 2X(x)\left(\mathit{log}X(x)+\mathit{log}(1+\mathbf{t}(x))-3\right),
$$

 whenever
$0<X(x)$

where, as in the statement of Theorem 4.26, $X(x)$ is the
maximum of zero and the negative of the smallest eigenvalue of $\mathit{Rm}(x)$.
