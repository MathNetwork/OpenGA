Let $\phi\colon B_{(q,t)}'\to \Ar$ be a non-negative, smooth
function with compact support. Then

$$
\int_{B_{(q,t)}'}l^\tau\triangle \phi d\mathit{vol}(g(t))\le \int_{(B_{(q,t)}')^*}\phi
\triangle l^\tau d\mathit{vol}(g(t)).
$$
