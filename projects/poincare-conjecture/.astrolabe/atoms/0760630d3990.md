For any $x\in \Omega\times\{T\}$ with $R(x,T)>r_0^{-2}$ one of the
following holds:

- **(1)** $(x,T)$ is the center of a strong $2\epsilon$-neck in
$(\widehat{\mathcal M},\widehat G)$.
- **(2)** There is a
$(2C,2\epsilon)$-cap in $(\Omega(T),\widehat G(T))$ whose core
contains $(x,T)$.
- **(3)** There is a $2C$-component of
$\Omega(T)$ that contains $(x,T)$.
- **(4)** There is a $2\epsilon$-round component of $\Omega(T)$ that
contains $(x,T)$.
