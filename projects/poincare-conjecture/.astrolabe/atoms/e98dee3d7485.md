We say that two minimizing geodesic rays limiting to ${\mathcal E}$
are *equivalent* if one is contained in the other. From the
unique continuation of geodesics it is easy to see that this
generates an equivalence relation. An equivalence class is a *direction at ${\mathcal E}$*, and the set of equivalence classes is
the *set of directions at ${\mathcal E}$*.
