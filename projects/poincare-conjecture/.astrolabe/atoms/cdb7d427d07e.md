Fix $C<\infty$ and $\epsilon>0$. For any Riemannian manifold
$(M,g)$, an open neighborhood $U$ of a point $x\in M$ is a *$(C,\epsilon)$-canonical neighborhood* if one of the following holds:

- **(1)** $U$ is an $\epsilon$-neck in $(M,g)$ centered at $x$.
- **(2)** $U$ a $(C,\epsilon)$-cap in $(M,g)$ whose core contains $x$.
- **(3)** $U$ is a $C$-component of $(M,g)$.
- **(4)** $U$ is an $\epsilon$-round component of $(M,g)$.
