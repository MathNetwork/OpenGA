Given any constant $c>0$ there is a constant $\widetilde
C=\widetilde C(c)$, depending only on $c$ and not on the
$3$-dimensional $\kappa$-solution, so that

$$
d_{g(-cQ^{-1})}(p,q)\le \tilde CQ^{-1/2}.
$$
