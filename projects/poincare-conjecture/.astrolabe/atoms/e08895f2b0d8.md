There is a non-negative constant $V_\infty<(4\pi)^{n/2}$ such that
for all $\tau\in(0,\infty)$, we have

$$
\mathit{lim}_{k\rightarrow\infty}\widetilde V_{x_k}(\tau)=V_\infty.
$$
