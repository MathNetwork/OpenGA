The *time-slices*
of space-time are the level sets $\mathbf{t}$. These form a
codimension-one foliation of ${\mathcal M}$. For each $t\in I$ we
denote by $M_t\subset {\mathcal M}$ the $t$ time-slice, that is to
say $\mathbf{t}^{-1}(t)$. Notice that each boundary component of
${\mathcal M}$ is contained in a single time-slice. The *horizontal distribution*, ${\mathcal H}T{\mathcal M}$ is the
distribution tangent to this foliation. A *horizontal
metric* on
space-time is a smoothly varying positive definite inner product on
${\mathcal H}T{\mathcal M}$.
