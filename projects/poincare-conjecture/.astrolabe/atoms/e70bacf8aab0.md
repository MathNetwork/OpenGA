For the given value of $q$ and for any $C_0$,
assuming that $\delta>0$ is sufficiently small, the smallest
eigenvalue of $\mathit{Rm}_{\hat h}$ is greater than the smallest eigenvalue
of $\mathit{Rm}_h$ at the same point. Consequently, at any point where $h$
has non-negative curvature so does $\hat h$.
