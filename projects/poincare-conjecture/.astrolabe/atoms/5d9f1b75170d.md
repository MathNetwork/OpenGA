Suppose that $(M,g(t)),\ -\infty<t<0$, is a connected, non-compact
$\kappa$-solution of dimension at most three (This result, in fact,
holds in all dimensions.). Then ${\mathcal R}(M,g(t))=+\infty$ for every
$t<0$.
