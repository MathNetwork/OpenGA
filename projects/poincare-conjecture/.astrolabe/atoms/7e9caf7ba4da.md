The *Ricci flow equation* is the
following evolution equation for a Riemannian metric:

$$
\frac{\partial g}{\partial t}= -2\mathit{Ric}(g).
$$

 A solution to
this equation (or a *Ricci flow*) is a one-parameter
family of metrics $g(t)$, parameterized by $t$ in a non-degenerate interval
$I$, on a smooth manifold $M$ satisfying Equation (3.1). If $I$ has
an initial point $t_0$ then $(M,g(t_0))$ is called *the initial condition
of* or *the initial metric for* the
Ricci flow (or of the solution).
