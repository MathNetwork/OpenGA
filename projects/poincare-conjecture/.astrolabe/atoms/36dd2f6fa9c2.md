If $(M,g(t))$ is a Ricci flow defined for $-\infty<t\le 0$ with
$(M,g(t))$ a complete manifold of bounded, non-negative curvature
operator for each $t$, then

$$
\frac{\partial R(x,t)}{\partial t}\ge 0.
$$
