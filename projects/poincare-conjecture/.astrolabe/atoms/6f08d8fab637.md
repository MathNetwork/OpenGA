Let $A\subset {\mathcal U}_x(\tau)$ be a measurable subset of
$M_{T-\tau}$. The $\mathcal{L}$-*reduced volume of $A$ from $x$*
(or the *reduced volume* for short) is
defined to be

$$
\widetilde V_x(A) = \int_{A} \tau^{-\frac{n}{2}}\mathit{exp}(-l_x(q))dq
$$

 where $dq$ is the volume
element of the metric $G(T-\tau)$.
