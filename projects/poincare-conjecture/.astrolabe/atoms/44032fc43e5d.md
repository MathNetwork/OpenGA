For any $0\le \tau_1<\tau_2<T$ suppose that
 $\gamma\colon [\tau_1,\tau_2]\to M\times [0,T]$ is an ${\mathcal L}$-geodesic.
 Then $\gamma$ extends uniquely to an ${\mathcal L}$-geodesic
$\gamma\colon [0,T)\to M\times (0,T]$.
