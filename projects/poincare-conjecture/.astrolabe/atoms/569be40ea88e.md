**(Ricci curvature comparison)** Fix $k\ge 0$. Assume that $(M,
g)$ satisfies $\mathit{Ric} \geq -(n-1)k$. Let $\gamma\colon [0,r_0)\to
M$ be a minimal geodesic of unit speed. Then for any $r<r_0$ at
$\gamma(r)$ we have

$$
\sqrt{\mathit{det} g(r,\theta)} \leq \mathit{sn}^{n-1}_{k}(r)
$$

 and

$$
\mathit{Tr}(S)(r,\theta) \leq (n-1)\frac{\mathit{sn}_{k}^{'}(r)}{\mathit{sn}_{k}(r)}.
$$
