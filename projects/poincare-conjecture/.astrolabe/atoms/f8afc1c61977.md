Suppose that $(M,g(t)),\ 0\le t\le T$, is a Ricci flow with $M$ a
compact, connected $3$-manifold with $\mathit{Ric}(x,0)\ge 0$ for all
$x\in M$. Then $\mathit{Ric}(x,t)\ge 0$ for all $t>0$.
