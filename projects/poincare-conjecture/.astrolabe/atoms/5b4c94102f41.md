Suppose that $\varphi$ and $\psi_k$ are as in Claim 9.27,
but in addition $\varphi$ and all the $\psi_k$ are uniformly
Lipschitz. Then

$$
\mathit{lim}_{k\rightarrow\infty}\int_{M_\infty}\varphi\psi_k\triangle
e^{-l_\infty}d\mathit{vol}_{g_\infty}
$$

 converges absolutely.
