Let $(M,g(t))$ be a $3$-dimensional $\kappa$-solution. Then one of
the following hold:

- **(1)** For every $t\le 0$ the manifold $(M,g(t))$ has positive
curvature.
- **(2)** $(M,g(t))$ is the product of an evolving family of
round $S^2$'s with a line.
- **(3)** $M$ is diffeomorphic to a line bundle over $\Ar P^2$, and
there is a finite covering of $(M,g(t))$ that is a flow as in (2).
