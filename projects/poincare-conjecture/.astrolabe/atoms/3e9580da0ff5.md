We have the following estimates:

$$
\begin{aligned}
(T'')^{3/2}-(T')^{3/2} & \ge &
\frac{1}{4}(T-T_{i-1})^{3/2} \\
4(T')^{3/2} & \le &  4(T-T_{i-1})^{3/2} \\
4\left((T-T_{i-1})^{3/2}-(T'')^{3/2}\right) & \le &
\frac{2t_0}{25}\sqrt{(T-T_{i-1})}.
\end{aligned}
$$
