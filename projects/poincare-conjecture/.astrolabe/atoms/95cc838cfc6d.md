Let $(M,g(t)),\ 0\le t<T$, be a Ricci flow with $M$ a compact
$n$-dimensional manifold. Denote by $R_\mathit{min}(t)$ the minimum
value of the scalar curvature of $(M,g(t))$. Then:

- $R_\mathit{min}(t)$ is a non-decreasing function
of $t$.
- If $R_\mathit{min}(0)\ge 0$, then

$$
R_\mathit{min}(t)\ge R_\mathit{min}(0)\left(\frac{1}{1-\frac{2t}{n}R_\mathit{min}(0)}\right),
$$

 in particular,

$$
T\le\frac{n}{2R_\mathit{min}(0)}.
$$

- If $R_\mathit{min}(0)<0$, then

$$
R_\mathit{min}(t)\ge
-\frac{n\bigl| R_\mathit{min}(0)\bigr|}{2t\bigl|R_\mathit{min}(0)\bigr|+n}.
$$
