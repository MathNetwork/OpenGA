(E.Calabi, 1958)
Let $f(x)=d(p,x)$ be the distance function from $p$. If $(M, g)$ has
$\mathit{Ric} \geq 0$, then

$$
\triangle f \leq \frac{n-1}{f}
$$

 in the sense of distributions.
