title: piecewise differentiable curve
---
A *piecewise differentiable curve* is a continuous map $c:[a,b]\to M$ for which
there is a partition $a=t_0<t_1<\dots<t_k=b$ such that each restriction
$c|_{[t_i,t_{i+1}]}$ is differentiable; $c$ *joins* $c(a)$ and $c(b)$. $c(t_i)$ is
a *vertex* of $c$, and the *vertex angle at $c(t_i)$* is the angle between
$\lim_{t\to t_i^+}c'(t)$ and $\lim_{t\to t_i^-}c'(t)$. Parallel transport extends
to such curves by transporting successively over each differentiable segment.
