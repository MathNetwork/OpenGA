title: the index form
---
The *index form* of $\gamma$ is the quadratic form associated to the symmetric
bilinear form $I_a$ defined on $\mathcal{V}$ by

$$
I_a(V,W)=\int_0^a\{\langle V',W'\rangle-\langle R(\gamma',V)\gamma',W\rangle\}\,dt,\qquad(1)
$$

where $V,W\in\mathcal{V}$ (cf. Rem. 2.10 of Chap. 9).

Observe that the symmetry of $I_a$ follows from part (d) of \entryref{91101fb52f8d}.

In general, given a symmetric bilinear form $B$ over a vector space
$\mathcal{V}$, we define the *index* of $B$ as the maximal dimension of all
subspaces of $\mathcal{V}$ on which the quadratic form associated to $B$ is
negative definite. The *nullity* of $B$ is defined to be the dimension of the
subspace of $\mathcal{V}$ formed by the elements $V\in\mathcal{V}$ such that
$B(V,W)=0$ for all $W\in\mathcal{V}$; such a subspace is called the *null space*
of $B$. We say that $B$ is *degenerate* if its nullity is strictly positive.
