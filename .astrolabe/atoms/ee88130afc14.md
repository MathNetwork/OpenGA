title: Minimizing curve is a geodesic
---
A piecewise-differentiable curve, parametrized proportionally to arc length, whose length is $\le$ that of any other curve joining its endpoints, is a **geodesic** (in particular regular). (do Carmo Ch 3, Cor 3.9.) This is the converse used in the Hopf–Rinow proof to conclude a broken minimizing path has no corner.
