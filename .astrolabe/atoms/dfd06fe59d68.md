title: injectivity radius
---
If $q\in M-C_m(p)$, there exists a unique minimizing geodesic joining $p$ to $q$.

Corollary 2.8 shows that $\exp_p$ is injective on the open ball $B_r(p)$ if and only
if $r\leq d(p,C_m(p))$. For this reason

$$
i(M)=\inf_{p\in M}d(p,C_m(p))
$$

is called the *injectivity radius of $M$*. Corollary 2.8 also shows that
$M-C_m(p)$ is homeomorphic to an open ball of Euclidean space; in a sense the
topology of $M$ is contained in its cut locus.
