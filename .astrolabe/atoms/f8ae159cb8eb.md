title: translation

An isometry $f:\tilde{M}\to\tilde{M}$ without fixed points is said to be a
*translation* of $\tilde{M}$ if it leaves invariant some geodesic $\tilde{\gamma}$
of $\tilde{M}$, that is, if $f(c)=c$, where $c=\tilde{\gamma}((-\infty,\infty))$.
In this case, we say that $f$ is a *translation along* $\tilde{\gamma}$.
