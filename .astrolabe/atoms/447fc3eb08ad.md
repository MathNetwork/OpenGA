title: Geodesics locally minimize
---
Let $U$ be a normal neighborhood of $p$ and $B\subset U$ a normal ball centered at $p$. A radial geodesic $\gamma:[0,1]\to B$ from $p$ is **minimizing**: $\ell(\gamma)\le\ell(c)$ for any piecewise-differentiable $c$ joining its endpoints, equality iff $c$ reparametrizes $\gamma$. Hence $d(\exp_p v,p)=\lVert v\rVert$ in a normal ball. (do Carmo Ch 3, Prop 3.6.)
