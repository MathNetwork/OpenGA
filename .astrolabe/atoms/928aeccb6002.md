By Synge's theorem (Chap. 9, Cor. 3.10) the manifolds satisfying the hypotheses of
\entryref{096001bed96a} are simply connected. Conversely a simply connected manifold is
orientable (else it has a two-sheeted covering, Exercise 12 of Chap. 0,
contradicting simple connectivity). Hence the orientability hypothesis in
\entryref{096001bed96a} is equivalent to assuming $M$ simply connected.
