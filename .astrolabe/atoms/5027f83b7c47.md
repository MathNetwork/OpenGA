title: isometry
---
Let $M,N$ be Riemannian manifolds. A diffeomorphism $f:M\to N$ is an *isometry* if

$$
\langle u,v\rangle_p=\langle df_p(u),df_p(v)\rangle_{f(p)},\qquad\text{(1)}
$$

for all $p\in M$, $u,v\in T_pM$.
