title: properties of the bracket

If $X,Y,Z$ are differentiable vector fields on $M$, $a,b\in\mathbb{R}$, and $f,g$
differentiable functions, then:

(a) $[X,Y]=-[Y,X]$ *(anticommutativity)*;

(b) $[aX+bY,Z]=a[X,Z]+b[Y,Z]$ *(linearity)*;

(c) $[[X,Y],Z]+[[Y,Z],X]+[[Z,X],Y]=0$ *(Jacobi identity)*;

(d) $[fX,gY]=fg[X,Y]+fX(g)Y-gY(f)X$.

*Proof.* (a),(b) are immediate. For (c), expand
$[[X,Y],Z]=XYZ-YXZ-ZXY+ZYX$ and
$[X,[Y,Z]]+[Y,[Z,X]]=XYZ-XZY-YZX+ZYX+YZX-YXZ-ZXY+XZY$; the second members
agree, and (c) follows using (a). For (d),
$[fX,gY]=fX(gY)-gY(fX)=fgXY+fX(g)Y-gfYX-gY(f)X=fg[X,Y]+fX(g)Y-gY(f)X$. $\square$

The bracket $[X,Y]$ can also be interpreted as a derivative of $Y$ along the
"trajectories" of $X$. Since a differentiable manifold is locally diffeomorphic
to $\mathbb{R}^n$, the fundamental theorem on existence, uniqueness and dependence
on initial conditions of ODEs extends naturally: for a differentiable vector
field $X$ and $p\in M$ there exist a neighborhood $U$ of $p$, an interval
$(-\delta,\delta)$, and a differentiable $\varphi:(-\delta,\delta)\times U\to M$
such that $t\mapsto\varphi(t,q)$ is the unique curve with
$\frac{\partial\varphi}{\partial t}=X(\varphi(t,q))$ and $\varphi(0,q)=q$. A curve
$\alpha$ with $\alpha'(t)=X(\alpha(t))$, $\alpha(0)=q$, is a *trajectory* of $X$;
$\varphi_t(q)=\varphi(t,q)$, $\varphi_t:U\to M$, is the *local flow* of $X$.
