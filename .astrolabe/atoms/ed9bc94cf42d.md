title: Hopf–Rinow Theorem
---
Let $(M^n, g)$ be a connected Riemannian manifold. Then $M$ is complete as a metric space if and only if it is geodesically complete. Equivalently, $\exp_p$ is defined on all of $T_pM$ for some (hence every) $p$, and any two points are joined by a minimizing geodesic.
