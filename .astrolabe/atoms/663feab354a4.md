title: flat normal bundle
---
We say the normal bundle of an immersion is *flat* if $R^\perp=0$. Assume the
ambient space $\overline{M}$ has constant sectional curvature. Then the Ricci
equation can be written

$$
\langle R^\perp(X,Y)\eta,\zeta\rangle=-\langle[S_\eta,S_\zeta]X,Y\rangle.
$$

It follows that $R^\perp=0$ if and only if $[S_\eta,S_\zeta]=0$ for all
$\eta,\zeta$, that is, if and only if for all $p\in M$ there exists a basis of
$T_pM$ which diagonalizes all of the $S_\eta$ simultaneously.

The Gauss and Ricci equations are algebraic relations between the curvatures of
the tangent and normal bundles and the second fundamental form. A non-algebraic
relation is the Codazzi equation, for which we differentiate the second
fundamental form considered as a tensor
$B:\mathcal{X}(M)\times\mathcal{X}(M)\times\mathcal{X}(M)^\perp\to\mathbb{R}$,
$B(X,Y,\eta)=\langle B(X,Y),\eta\rangle$, with covariant derivative

$$
(\overline{\nabla}_X B)(Y,Z,\eta)=X(B(Y,Z,\eta))-B(\nabla_X Y,Z,\eta)-B(Y,\nabla_X Z,\eta)-B(Y,Z,\nabla_X^\perp\eta).
$$
