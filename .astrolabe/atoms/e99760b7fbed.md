title: topology coincides
---
The topology induced by $d$ on $M$ coincides with the original topology on $M$.
*Proof.* From the remark above, if $r$ is sufficiently small the normal ball
$B_r(p)$ coincides with the metric ball of radius $r$ centered at $p$. Hence
metric balls contain normal balls, and conversely. $\square$
