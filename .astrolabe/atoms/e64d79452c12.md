title: Riemannian distance
---
$d_g(p,q) = \inf_\gamma L(\gamma)$, the infimum of lengths $L(\gamma)=\int \lVert \dot\gamma \rVert\,dt$ over piecewise-smooth curves from $p$ to $q$. It makes $(M, d_g)$ a metric space whose topology is the manifold topology.
