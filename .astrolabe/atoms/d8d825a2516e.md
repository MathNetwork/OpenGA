Fix $p, q$ and set $r = d(p, q)$. On a compact geodesic sphere $S_\delta$ choose $x_0 = \exp_p(\delta v_0)$ minimizing $d(\cdot, q)$, and follow $\gamma(s)=\exp_p(s v_0)$; the set $A=\{s: d(\gamma(s),q)=r-s\}$ is closed, contains $\delta$, and is open upward, hence equals $[\delta,r]$, so $\gamma(r)=q$ along a minimizing segment. (do Carmo, Ch. 7.)
