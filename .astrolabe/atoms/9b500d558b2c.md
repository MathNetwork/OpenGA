title: the cut locus is closed

For all $p\in M$, $C_m(p)$ is closed; in particular, if $M$ is compact, $C_m(p)$ is
compact.

*Proof.* One verifies $C_m(p)=\{\gamma(t);t=f(p,\gamma'(0))\}$, where $f$ is the
function of \entryref{31ea0ff2d398}. If $q$ is an accumulation point of $C_m(p)$, take
$\gamma_j(t_j)\to q$ with $t_j=f(p,\gamma_j'(0))$ and $\gamma_j'(0)\to v$. With
$\gamma(0)=p$, $\gamma'(0)=v$, continuity of $f$ gives
$q=\exp_p(f(p,\gamma'(0))\gamma'(0))=\gamma(f(p,\gamma'(0)))\in C_m(p)$. $\square$
