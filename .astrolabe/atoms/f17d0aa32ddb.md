title: change of parameters as an axiom
---
Comparison with the definition of a regular surface in $\mathbb{R}^3$ shows that
the essential point (besides the change of dimension from $2$ to $n$) was to
isolate the fundamental property of the change of parameters — a *theorem* for
surfaces in $\mathbb{R}^3$ — and incorporate it as condition (2) of \entryref{e393a9b2a18c}. This is the condition that lets all of the ideas of differential calculus
in $\mathbb{R}^n$ carry over to differentiable manifolds.
