title: Gauss Lemma
---
For $p\in M$, $v\in T_pM$ with $\exp_p v$ defined, and $w\in T_pM\cong T_v(T_pM)$: $\langle (d\exp_p)_v v,\,(d\exp_p)_v w\rangle=\langle v,w\rangle$. So $d\exp_p$ is a **radial isometry** and geodesic spheres are orthogonal to radial geodesics. Proved elementarily from the symmetry lemma — **no Jacobi fields**. (do Carmo Ch 3, Lemma 3.5.)
