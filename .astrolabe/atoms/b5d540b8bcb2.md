title: Totally normal neighborhood
---
For any $p\in M$ there are $W\ni p$ and $\delta>0$ such that for every $q\in W$, $\exp_q$ is a diffeomorphism on $B_\delta(0)\subset T_qM$ with $\exp_q(B_\delta(0))\supseteq W$. Any two points of $W$ are joined by a unique minimizing geodesic of length $<\delta$, depending smoothly on the endpoints. (do Carmo Ch 3, Thm 3.7 + Rmk 3.8.)
