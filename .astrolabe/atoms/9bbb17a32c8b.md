For various reasons, it is convenient to identify the field $X\in\mathcal{X}(M)$
with the tensor $X:\mathcal{X}(M)\to\mathcal{D}(M)$ given by $X(Y)=\langle X,Y\rangle$,
for all $Y\in\mathcal{X}(M)$.
