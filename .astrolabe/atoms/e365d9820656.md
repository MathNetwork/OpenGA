title: natural topology
---
A differentiable structure on $M$ induces a natural topology: define $A\subset M$
to be *open* if and only if $\mathbf{x}_\alpha^{-1}(A\cap\mathbf{x}_\alpha(U_\alpha))$
is open in $\mathbb{R}^n$ for all $\alpha$. One checks that $M$ and $\phi$ are
open, arbitrary unions and finite intersections of open sets are open, the sets
$\mathbf{x}_\alpha(U_\alpha)$ are open and the mappings $\mathbf{x}_\alpha$ are
continuous. Euclidean space $\mathbb{R}^n$ with the structure given by the
identity is the trivial example of a differentiable manifold.
