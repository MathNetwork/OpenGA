title: Corollary 3.7
---
If $\langle J,\gamma'\rangle(t_1)=\langle J,\gamma'\rangle(t_2)$, $t_1,t_2\in[0,a]$,
$t_1\neq t_2$, then $\langle J,\gamma'\rangle$ does not depend on $t$; in
particular, if $J(0)=J(a)=0$, then $\langle J,\gamma'\rangle(t)\equiv 0$.
