title: conjugate point, multiplicity
---
Let $\gamma:[0,a]\to M$ be a geodesic. The point $\gamma(t_0)$ is said to be
*conjugate* to $\gamma(0)$ along $\gamma$, $t_0\in(0,a]$, if there exists a Jacobi
field $J$ along $\gamma$, not identically zero, with $J(0)=0=J(t_0)$. The maximum
number of such linearly independent fields is called the *multiplicity* of the
conjugate point $\gamma(t_0)$.

Observe that if $\gamma(t_0)$ is conjugate to $\gamma(0)$, then $\gamma(0)$ is
conjugate to $\gamma(t_0)$.
